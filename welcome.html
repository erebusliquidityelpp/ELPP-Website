The bot

Discipline, automated.

The same desk, with a Telegram bot that scans, guards risk and reports back.

  • Analysis. Scans 30 to 60 coins using SMC, ICT, multi-timeframe, divergence and order flow.
  • Secondary Test Reclaim. No entry on the first sweep, only on a confirmed retest.
  • Real exchange orders for stops, trailing stops and partial take-profit. Breakeven is moved manually.
  • Pre-trade risk gate covering correlation, exposure and position limits, with volatility-adjusted sizing, emergency close-all and exchange resync.
  • Market regime. Detects trending, ranging or volatile conditions and weights setups.
  • Options and derivatives. Put/call, max pain and DVOL for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, AVAX and TRX.
  • News and cross-checks. Live news, regulatory matching, Binance cross-checks, daily and weekly briefs, economic calendar and journal.
  • Whales and arbitrage. Whale trades, wallet watching and a funding-rate arbitrage scanner.

Questions, rules and risk.

Read this before you join.

Does ELPP trade for me?

No. The desk is analysis only and the website never places trades. ELPP and NEK Global Holdings will never ask you to trade for them or request keys, seed phrases or funds.

The scanner app also has an optional trading menu that starts in paper mode and works only with your own trade-only keys on your own device. Use read-only API keys wherever you can, and never keys with withdrawal permission.

What do I need to start?

Sign up, start the 7-day testnet trial with /trial in the bot, and explore the full desk. When you are ready, pay for a plan and send the proof in the bot.

How are alerts delivered?

You set them on the website and they arrive through your own Telegram bot. The server checks every minute and applies cooldowns.
