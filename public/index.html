Data options

Open interest and funding have no history on Hyperliquid, so their changes are measured from samples this page collects and saves in your browser. Each timeframe starts filling in after that much time has passed. Order-book rules (imbalance, spread) fetch one snapshot per coin only while such a rule is switched on.

What the newer filters mean

Range position: where price sits between the low (0) and high (100) of the window.

Sweep + reclaim: the timeframe is the size of the range. +1 means price recently dipped below that range's low and is back above it; -1 means it poked above the high and is back below. "Recently" follows the lookback above.

Timeframe agreement: +3 if 15m, 1h and 4h all moved up, -3 if all down.

Distance from VWAP: "day" is the VWAP since 00:00 UTC. Below high / above low: distance to the window's extremes.

ATR %: 14-bar average true range as a share of price, on clock-aligned bars. RVOL best of last 3: the strongest of the last three closed bars against the average bar.

Change vs BTC: the coin's move minus BTC's move over the same window, in percentage points. Order book imbalance: (bids − asks) ÷ (bids + asks) within 1% of the mid price; it flips quickly and can be spoofed.

Net money flow: for each 5m candle, volume × where the close sits in the candle's range (close at the high = all buying, at the low = all selling), added up over the window. It is an estimate from candles, not true taker data and not exchange deposits and withdrawals. Flow % is the flow divided by total volume, from −100% to +100%.

Imbalance %: (bids − asks) ÷ (bids + asks) in the order book within 1% of price. Positive means more resting buy orders. Buy/sell pressure: 60% net money flow plus 40% book imbalance (flow only until the book loads); +15 or more reads as buying, −15 or less as selling.

Regime (last 4h): Manipulation = a swept high or low that price reclaimed. Chopping = price goes nowhere (efficiency under 0.25) with no clear flow. Accumulation = sideways, in the lower half of the range, with net buying. Distribution = sideways, in the upper half, with net selling. Trending = a directional move. Trend: Up or Down needs a move beyond the timeframe's noise band and efficiency of at least 0.25; otherwise Consolidating.

Liquidity: dollars resting in the order book within 1% of price, both sides. Over $2M is deep, $300k to $2M is ok, under $300k is thin. Liquidity % is that depth as a share of open interest. Book data needs one extra request per coin, so it fills in over a minute or two.

Take-off score (0 to 100, long and short scored separately): coiling price (15), phase (20: accumulation or a swept low for longs, distribution or a swept high for shorts), pressure (20), higher-timeframe trend (10), positioning and funding (10), volume waking up (10), BTC not against it (5). Minus points if the coin already ran far (late) or its order book is thin. 60 or more means the set-up is ready; it does not tell you the time of the breakout. It needs Hyperliquid candle data, so coins that only trade elsewhere show a dash.

Build-up: the coin is in accumulation, distribution or a stop hunt and buying or selling pressure is already building in the same direction. That is the list to prepare entries for after the breakout.

Set-ups

Changes are measured against 5-minute bars (so 15m = 3 bars back). RVOL = volume in the window ÷ the average of earlier windows of the same length over about 48 hours. Volatility = high–low range of the window. BTC correlation uses returns over 24 h (15m) or 48 h (1h). The bias column compares price and open-interest direction on the Bias timeframe; it is a hint to look closer, not a signal. Click a score badge to see which rules passed.
